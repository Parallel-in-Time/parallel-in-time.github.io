The [$2^{2^2}$th Workshop on Parallel-in-Time Integration (PinT 2027)](/events/16th-pint-workshop) will be hosted at [Jilin University](https://en.jlu.edu.cn/), Changchun, China, from **19. to 23. July 2027**.

It is organized by the [Tianyuan Mathematical Center in Northeast China](https://tianyuanmc.jlu.edu.cn/) and hosted at the Jilin National Application Mathematics Center.

Abstract submission and registration deadline is **Monday, April 19, 2027**; notification of acceptance follows on May 19, 2027.
